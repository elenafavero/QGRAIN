UNIVERSITY OF PADOVA – Department of Industrial Engineering
Interim report – Research agreement with Termovia S.p.A. – June 2026

Scope: reduction of fan noise in the outdoor unit of air-to-water heat pumps.
Measurements in the semi-anechoic chamber show a 3 dB(A) reduction with the new fan blade profile.
The interaction between the fan and the outdoor heat exchanger remains the main noise source.
Next step: tests on the complete unit in September.
